```markdown
# Comprehensive Analysis of Grant Programs & Failure Modes
*Prepared for Optimism Collective — S6 Delegate Mission Request*

## 1. Scope and Objectives
- **Objective:** Analyze past grant programs across Optimism and broader Web3 ecosystems to identify common failure modes, success factors, and structural bottlenecks.
- **Target Audience:** Optimism Grants Council, Token House delegates, and emerging Superchain participants establishing their own grant mechanisms.

## 2. Research Methodology & Data Collection
- **Quantitative Metrics:**
  - Analysis of >20 distinct grant cohorts (Optimism Seasons 1–5, Arbitrum DAO, Gitcoin Grants, Uniswap Grants).
  - Tracking fund utilization rates, milestone completion percentages, and post-grant token retention/protocol activity.
- **Qualitative Metrics:**
  - Structured interviews with 15+ former grant program operators, reviewers, and recipients.
  - Review of governance forum discussions, post-mortems, and dispute resolution logs.

## 3. Failure Mode Taxonomy
1. **Adverse Selection & Information Asymmetry:** Grantees promising unrealistic metrics without verifiable on-chain commitments.
2. **Milestone Verification Gaps:** Subjective milestone evaluations leading to fund disbursement without adequate accountability.
3. **Contributor Burnout & Volunteer Fatigue:** Over-reliance on elected council members without institutional tooling or dedicated operations support.
4. **Superchain Fragmented Silos:** Lack of shared intelligence across emerging L2/Superchain grant programs leading to repeated mistakes.

## 4. Evaluation Framework & Deliverables
- **The Grant Health Scorecard (GHS):** A standardized heuristic for future grant programs to evaluate proposals across governance risk, capital efficiency, and decentralization impact.
- **Collective DAO Archives Report:** A published, open-access research document and slide deck presented to Token House delegates.
```
